- **Fixed for Questie 12.8 and later.** Questie moved its map pins into an internal module, so D2Map could no longer find them and silently fell back to the game's own quest markers (one per quest). The pins are back.
- **Mining veins.** The map now shows where ore can spawn, as small coloured diamonds (copper orange, tin grey, silver white, iron blue, gold yellow, mithril light blue, thorium green). By default you only see the veins your Mining skill can handle, and nothing at all on a character without Mining. Pick "All veins" or "Off" under `/d2map` (or `/d2map ore <mine|all|off>`). The spots come from Questie's database, so this needs Questie. They are spawn *places*: the game does not tell addons which veins are up right now.
