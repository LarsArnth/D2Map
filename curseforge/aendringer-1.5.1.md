First public release.

- Quest pins from Questie around your character, with a rim marker for quests whose targets are off the map
- A soft outline around each cluster of quest objectives
- The guide path from Factoruide as a bright dotted line
- Optional terrain from the game's own minimap tiles (off by default)
- North up, or rotating with the way you face
- Separate opacity for standing still, moving and combat
- `/d2map` opens the options window; key bindings for toggle and zoom
